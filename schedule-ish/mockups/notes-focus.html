Shared focus notes (mockup)

Click a block to open its notes. All three Job applications blocks share one focus list: tick something on Monday and it's ticked on Wednesday. Gym and Lunch just hold time, so they have nothing in them, and that's fine. Nothing here is saved.

In the dialog: the top unticked line is what's next. Drag lines by the ⋮⋮ grip to change what comes first. Tick a line and it folds away into "done this week". Enter starts a new line; Backspace on an empty line removes it. "This session" is an optional line just for the block you opened.
